RoboDoc

A doctor assistive robot to isolate patient and doctor in the situation of COVID-19 pandemic. Equipped with sensors for real-time patient vitals monitoring, vision algorithms for facial expression detection, and a dashboard for detailed data visualization, streamlining patient care for medical professionals.

Key Responsibilities

  • Designed and developed firmware for robot locomotion, haptic feedback, and control of the robot's onboard actions.
  • Developed computer vision algorithms for action recognition, sentiment analysis, and object detection to support real-time patient monitoring.
  • Integrated sensors and camera modules with the onboard microprocessor using BLE and MQTT protocols for real-time data transmission to the dashboard.
  • Designed and integrated a 7-degree-of-freedom robotic arm with sensor feedback for precise, controlled interaction with patients.
  • Designed the robot's power management system to support reliable, extended operation.
  • Deployed the robot in a local hospital and trained medical staff on its operation, enabling safer doctor-patient interaction during the COVID-19 pandemic.
  • Runner-up, Ali Suleman Habib 2nd Engineering Excellence Award, for this project.
