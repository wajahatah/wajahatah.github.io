Chest Trauma Detection

A computer vision system to detect and classify chest trauma from X-ray imagery, developed in collaboration with Aga Khan University Hospital to support faster, more consistent diagnostic screening.

Key Responsibilities

  • Trained a custom 4-layer 3D CNN for chest X-ray image classification and fine-tuned ResNet18 on domain-specific datasets, improving diagnostic classification performance.
  • Designed and curated a labeled X-ray image dataset for training and evaluating the classification models.
  • Collaborated with Aga Khan University Hospital to align detection categories and evaluation criteria with clinical diagnostic needs.
  • Evaluated model performance across multiple architectures to identify the best trade-off between classification accuracy and inference speed.
