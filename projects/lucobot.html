LucoBot

A receptionist robot to entertain guests and be a bridge between guest and host

Key Responsibilities

  • Developed firmware for robot locomotion and a companion mobile application, built with MIT App Inventor, for guest interaction.
  • Integrated sensors for real-time obstacle avoidance during autonomous locomotion.
  • Developed and integrated a person re-identification and facial recognition pipeline, enabling the robot to recognize and interact with guests.
  • Designed the robot's locomotion base, power management system, and complete sensor wiring harness for reliable operation.
